Privacy Policy

Lazarus Arcade games · Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy covers all games published by Lazarus Arcade (the "games"), including Echo Bat.

What we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell your personal information. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data. Game progress (such as high scores and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The games are free and show ads through third-party ad networks. Each game uses one of: Google AdMob, AppLovin MAX, or Unity LevelPlay. These networks may collect device and usage information, such as your IP address, device model, OS version, ad interactions, coarse location derived from IP, and — only if you allow tracking — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), to show, measure, and limit ads and to prevent fraud.

  • On iOS, the game asks permission before any tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalized.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), a consent form lets you choose how your data is used for ads.

Ad network privacy policies: Google · AppLovin · Unity

Children

The games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your privacy choices

You can turn off tracking at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, reset your advertising identifier, or delete the game to remove all locally stored data.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can accept, refuse or choose per purpose. In US states with privacy laws (such as California), letting the ad network use your data for interest-based ads can count as "selling" or "sharing" it; you can opt out. In both cases, tap Privacy choices in the game's menu or Settings to change your decision at any time. You can also exercise your rights (access, deletion, correction) directly with the ad network linked above, because we do not hold your data ourselves.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: see Support.
