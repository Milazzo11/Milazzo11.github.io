Support

Questions, bugs, or feedback about any Lazarus Arcade game? Open an issue at github.com/Milazzo11/Milazzo11.github.io/issues.

FAQ

How do I stop personalized ads? On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn the game off. You will still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

Is my progress saved? High scores are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.
